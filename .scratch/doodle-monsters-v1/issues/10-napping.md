# 10: Napping

**What to build:** If a kid leaves in the middle of a Catch (the app is hidden, closed or navigated away from), the Monster is saved as Napping. It waits in the Zoo with a 💤, and tapping it resumes the Catch. Nothing the kid made is lost.

**Blocked by:** 09 (Zoo).

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A Napping Monster shows in the Zoo with a 💤
- [ ] Tapping a Napping Monster resumes its Catch (Dizzy can start fresh)
- [ ] Catching a resumed Monster makes it a normal Zoo Monster
- [ ] Core tests: "leaving a Catch leaves the Monster Napping in the Zoo", "resuming returns to the Catch", "Napping survives reloading from storage"
