# Glossary

**Monster**: a creature a kid drew, brought to life in the game. Each Monster is drawn once, caught once, and then lives in its kid's Zoo. It's never destroyed.
_Avoid_: enemy, boss, sprite

**Googly Eyes**: stickers a kid places on their drawing (1 to 5 of them). They jiggle when the Monster moves and follow the kid's finger.

**Catch**: the play phase where a kid wears a Monster down until it's caught. Catching never hurts or destroys the Monster, and each Monster is caught exactly once.
_Avoid_: defeat, kill, fight, beat

**Bonk**: a tap that lands on a moving Monster. It makes the Monster more Dizzy.

**Flick**: a quick swipe across a moving Monster that flings it into a wall. It counts as two Bonks.

**Voice**: how a Monster's sounds are pitched, a little higher or lower than everyone else's. It's chosen once, so the Monster always sounds like itself.

**Stunned**: the short moment after a Bonk when the Monster wobbles in place and can't be Bonked again.

**Dizzy**: how worn out a Monster is during a Catch. It fades if the Monster goes too long without a Bonk. When a Monster is fully Dizzy, one more tap catches it.

**Temperament**: the personality a kid picks for their Monster from a short list. It decides how the Monster behaves during its Catch and shows up in its Bio.
_Avoid_: difficulty, class, type

**Trick**: a Temperament's signature move during a Catch, such as fading out or dodging a tap. A Trick can fool a kid but never undoes their Dizzy. The Monster uses it more often as it gets more Dizzy, and stops once it's sleepy.
_Avoid_: power, special, attack

**Bio**: a short, silly description of a Monster, written from what it looks like and its Temperament. It appears on the Monster's Name Card.
_Avoid_: description, story

**Zoo**: one kid's collection of every Monster they've caught, all still alive and moving. Each kid has their own Zoo.
_Avoid_: gallery, trophy case, collection

**Say Goodbye**: a kid choosing to remove a Monster from their Zoo for good. The Monster waves and walks away. It's the only way a Monster ever leaves.
_Avoid_: delete, release, remove

**Napping**: a Monster whose Catch was interrupted. It waits in the Zoo until the kid comes back to finish catching it.

**Habitat**: the scene where Zoo Monsters wander around. A large Zoo is split into several Habitats.
_Avoid_: page, enclosure, grid

**Name Card**: the details shown when a kid taps a Monster in the Zoo, such as its name.

**Round**: one full pass of drawing a Monster, catching it, and seeing it move into the Zoo.
_Avoid_: level, game, match
